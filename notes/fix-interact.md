## For other areas

- **UI (Inspector)**: the size sliders can make a wall item taller than its wall; `editor.placement.constrain({ ...item, dims })`
  returns `status`/`message` («Høyere enn veggen – kappes til 231 cm», «For høy for veggen») and `dims` (cut) – the
  slider could show «kappes til …» or clamp `h`. (`flow-a` of the integrator now times out on
  `.ipi-swap .kv-iconbtn[title="Neste modell"]` – the Inspector's swap buttons changed; not an INTERACT issue.)
- **HUD**: ViewModes hides `hud.compass` with `style.visibility` outside walk view; a `hud.setCompassVisible()` would be
  cleaner if the HUD owner wants it.
- **Integrator / Editor.raycast**: could use `intersectFast` from Selection.js for the same speed-up.
- **LAMPS**: nothing needed for the arc lamp (its `colliders` are used); any new overhanging model should give
  `colliders` (base + overhang) so placement does not use its whole box.
